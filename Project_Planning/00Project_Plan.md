# Telco Customer Churn Prediction
**Date:** September 21, 2026

## Project Overview
This project focuses on the development of a predictive analytics solution to address customer attrition within the telecommunications sector. By analyzing account and usage patterns, we aim to identify customers at high risk of canceling their services (churning). Early identification enables stakeholders—including company owners and executive leadership—to implement proactive retention strategies, thereby safeguarding recurring revenue and improving long-term customer lifetime value.

The analysis leverages the "Telco Customer Churn" dataset, consisting of over 7,000 records detailing demographic attributes (gender, senior status) and service characteristics (tenure, internet/phone service, security add-ons). We will utilize advanced machine learning techniques, including classification and regression analysis, with performance success measured by F1-score and Precision-Recall Area Under Curve (PR-AUC).
