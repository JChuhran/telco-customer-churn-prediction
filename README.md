**Course:** IT4793 Capstone Project  
**Semester:** Fall 2026  
**Institution:** Kennesaw State University - College of Computing and Software Engineering (CCSE)

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Our project addresses customer churn in the telecommunications industry. By applying predictive analytics and machine learning techniques to customer demographic and service usage data, we identify at-risk customers to support targeted retention efforts and safeguard recurring business revenue.

- **Dataset:** Kaggle Telco Customer Churn (~7,000+ unique records)
- **Analytical Approach:** Predictive modeling (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost)
- **Primary Success Metrics:** F1-Score & PR-AUC (Precision-Recall Area Under Curve)
