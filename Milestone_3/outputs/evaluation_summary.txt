TELCO CUSTOMER CHURN - MODEL EVALUATION SUMMARY
=============================================

Results at the Best-F1 threshold:

Highest F1-Score: Random Forest (0.6219)
Highest PR-AUC: XGBoost (0.6590)
Highest Recall: LightGBM (0.8021)
Fewest Missed Churners: LightGBM (74)
Fewest False Alarms: Logistic Regression (M2 baseline) (229)

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION
-----------------------
Higher recall helps identify more customers at risk of leaving, but may also increase false alarms and unnecessary retention costs.
The best model depends on the company's priorities and the cost of missed churners compared with unnecessary interventions.

THRESHOLD ANALYSIS
------------------
Random Forest achieved an F1-score of 0.6219 at its Best-F1 threshold and 0.6341 at its recall-target threshold.
The recall-target threshold reduced false alarms from 262 to 214 but increased missed churners from 87 to 101.
This demonstrates the trade-off between identifying customers at risk and avoiding unnecessary interventions.

CONCLUSION
----------
Random Forest is a promising candidate because of its competitive F1-score and flexible threshold performance.
XGBoost achieved the highest PR-AUC, while LightGBM identified the largest proportion of actual churners at the Best-F1 operating point.
Final model selection should consider business costs and performance on future unseen data.
